# 🎬 Basics of Video Editing

## 📌 What is Video Editing
Video editing is the process of manipulating and arranging video clips to create a final video.

This includes:
- Cutting unwanted parts
- Arranging clips in sequence
- Adding audio, effects, and transitions
- Exporting the final result

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters
Understanding the basics helps you:
- Work faster and more efficiently
- Avoid common beginner mistakes
- Build a strong foundation for advanced editing

---

## ✂️ Core Concepts

### 1. Timeline
The timeline is the main workspace where you arrange your video.

- Left → beginning of video
- Right → end of video
- Multiple tracks:
  - Video track
  - Audio track

👉 Think of it as a “canvas” where your story is built.

---

### 2. Clip
A clip is a piece of video or audio.

Examples:
- A full recorded video
- A small cut from a larger video

---

### 3. Cut
Cut means splitting a clip into parts.

Used for:
- Removing mistakes
- Changing scenes
- Improving pacing

---

### 4. Trim
Trim means adjusting the start or end of a clip.

Used for:
- Removing unnecessary frames
- Making transitions smoother

---

## 📐 Resolution & Frame Rate

### Resolution
Resolution defines the size of the video.

Common resolutions:
- 720p (HD)
- 1080p (Full HD)
- 4K (Ultra HD)

👉 Higher resolution = sharper image but heavier file

---

### Frame Rate (FPS)
Frame rate = number of frames per second.

Common values:
- 24 fps → cinematic look
- 30 fps → standard video
- 60 fps → smooth motion

👉 Higher FPS = smoother motion but larger file size

---

## 📦 Video Formats

### MP4
- Most common format
- High compatibility (phones, browsers, YouTube)
- Good balance between quality and size

---

### MKV
- Flexible format
- Supports multiple audio and subtitles
- Often used for high-quality video storage

---

### MOV
- Developed by Apple
- High quality
- Common in professional workflows

---

## 💡 Practical Tips
- Start editing with 1080p resolution
- Use 30 fps for general content
- Use MP4 (H.264) for exporting videos
- Focus on clean cuts before adding effects

---

## 🚀 Summary
- Video editing = arranging and refining clips into a final video
- Timeline is your main workspace
- Resolution and FPS affect quality and performance
- MP4 is the safest format for most use cases
